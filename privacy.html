Privacy Policy — Invoice PDF to Excel (offline)

Last updated: 2026-10-10

Your invoices never leave your computer.

  • The extension reads only the PDF files you explicitly select or drop into its page and converts them into a table inside your browser, using code bundled in the extension (pdf.js, Apache-2.0).
  • It makes no network requests: its content security policy forbids connections and it loads no remote code.
  • It requests no browser permissions and cannot access websites you visit, tabs, history or downloads.
  • It collects no analytics, identifiers or telemetry and stores nothing persistently; extracted data exists in memory only until you close or clear the page.
  • We do not sell, share, rent or transfer user data to anyone, and do not use it for any purpose other than converting the files you choose.
  • Invoices may contain third parties' personal data (names, bank accounts). Processing happens only on your device; the publisher never receives it.
  • To delete everything: close the extension page or uninstall the extension.
